Frequently Asked Questions

Does it support milliseconds?
Yes, enter a timestamp with milliseconds (e.g. 1693526400123) and it will be parsed correctly.
What timezone is used?
The converter uses your browser's local timezone. ISO 8601 output is in UTC.
Can I copy the result?
Yes, results are displayed in text fields that you can select and copy.
